# Green Growth Site

Build a modern, sleek, high-converting one-page website for a local gardening and property maintenance business called "Harry Green Gardening."

REAL BUSINESS INFO (use only this, do not invent facts, testimonials, reviews, years of experience, or team size):

- Business name: Harry Green Gardening

- Services offered:

1. Property maintenance

2. Grounds maintenance

3. Garden maintenance

4. Exterior painting and repairs

- Tagline to use: "No job too big or small"

- Phone number: +44 7869 105848

- Encourage visitors to call/text/WhatsApp directly "Don't hesitate to get in contact"

DESIGN DIRECTION:

- Style: modern, clean, premium, a little bold and "sexy" confident typography, generous white space, smooth scroll animations, subtle hover effects on buttons/cards

- Color palette: deep forest green, warm off-white/cream, and one accent color (e.g. burnt orange or gold) for CTAs

- Mobile-first, fully responsive

- Big, clear, sticky "Call Now" button (tel:+447869105848) always visible, especially on mobile

- Use elegant, natural typography (serif for headings, clean sans-serif for body) to feel premium, not generic

PAGE STRUCTURE (single scrolling page):

1. HERO SECTION

- Business name "Harry Green Gardening" large and bold

- Short punchy subheadline describing what he does (property, grounds, garden maintenance, and exterior painting/repairs) written to sound trustworthy and capable, not exaggerated

- Primary CTA button: "Call Now" linking to tel:+447869105848

- use a simple clean placeholder/illustration or neutral stock nature/garden texture background instead 4k

2. SERVICES SECTION

- 4 clean cards/icons, one per service:

- Property Maintenance

- Grounds Maintenance

- Garden Maintenance

- Exterior Painting & Repairs

- Each card: short 1-2 sentence description of what that service typically involves in general terms (e.g. "Keeping outdoor spaces tidy, safe, and well-kept all year round") do not claim specific results, specific past clients, or numbers Harry hasn't provided

3. "WHY CONTACT HARRY" SECTION

- Instead of fake testimonials, write this as an honest value-proposition section, e.g.:

- Direct, personal contact, no call centers, no middlemen

- Flexible for jobs of any size

- Local, hands-on tradesman

- Clearly do NOT include any quotes, star ratings, or "customer reviews"

5. CONTACT / CTA SECTION

- Large, prominent phone number: +44 7869 105848 (clickable tel: and a "Tap to Call" button)

- Line: "Don't hesitate to get in contact"

- Optional simple contact form (Name, Message) that sends to email/phone — no fake "response time" promises

- WhatsApp click-to-chat button using the same number

6. FOOTER

- Business name, phone number, and a small note like "© [current year] Harry Green Gardening. All rights reserved."

- No fake address, no fake social links unless real ones are provided

IMPORTANT RULES:

- Do not fabricate testimonials, review scores, years in business, number of clients, certifications, or awards.

- Do not use AI-generated images and present them as real photos of Harry's actual work, use neutral placeholders or generic royalty-free nature/garden textures clearly marked as placeholders.

- Keep all copy honest, warm, and professional, confident but not exaggerated or clickbaity.

- Make the phone number impossible to miss on every screen size. make it 2026 and above theme it should be very simple too yet perfect DO NOT ADD DASHES LIKE THIS - BETWEEN WORDS
